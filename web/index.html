Learning centre

Simple guide to the math behind shaking buildings.

Why use matrices?

Instead of one equation per floor, M (weights) and K (stiffness) pack the whole building into two neat grids.

K · u = f

What are modes & frequencies?

Each mode is a natural sway pattern. The eigenvalue gives sway speed, and the eigenvector gives the bent shape.

(K − ω²M) φ = 0

Why modes stay independent

Mode shapes are orthogonal under M and K, turning coupled grids into clean diagonal numbers.

Φᵀ M Φ = I , Φᵀ K Φ = Λ

Splitting an earthquake into simple steps

Using mode coordinates solves each mode like a single pendulum, then adds them back together.

q̈ᵢ + 2ζωᵢq̇ᵢ + ωᵢ²qᵢ = −Γᵢ ü_g(t)
